There is a problem with this person's account

This person's account may not be set up correctly or they may no longer have access to this service.

What you need to do

Check their access roles

Ask a local systems administrator to confirm the user has been given the Allocations - POM role on Digital Prison Services.

Ensure they have been added to this service

Go to the Manage staff section to see if this person is listed as either a prison POM or a probation POM. If not, add them as a POM.

Check the Attention needed tab

If they are listed on the Attention needed tab of the Manage staff section, you must reallocate their cases so they can be removed from this service.

Change their status

If this person has left the prison either permanently or temporarily, select their name in the Manage staff section and then select edit profile to change their status.
